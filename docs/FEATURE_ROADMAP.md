# Portfolio feature roadmap

This is a client-services portfolio, not a SaaS dashboard. Features should help a qualified buyer understand proof, scope, and the next safe step. Impact and effort are estimates; validate the problem before building. Existing search, project filters, share links, résumé download, contact actions, and consent-gated LeadHub form are baseline features.

| # | Feature | User problem solved | Alternative gap to test | Impact | Effort | Acceptance proof |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence cards for each project | Buyers cannot tell what actually shipped | Marketplaces compress case proof into profiles | High | Medium | Every claim links to source/demo and is owner-verified |
| 2 | Scope-fit selector | Buyers do not know whether work fits the service offer | Listings are often broad or underspecified | High | Medium | Choice routes to a relevant, non-binding enquiry prompt |
| 3 | Paid discovery brief | Buyers fear open-ended consulting | Marketplaces encourage comparing bids before clarifying need | High | Medium | Scope, deliverable, exclusions, and next step are explicit; price owner-approved |
| 4 | Availability and response-time status | Buyers cannot tell if an enquiry will be answered | Platform profiles rarely show direct capacity | High | Low | Owner-controlled status with last-updated date; no auto-promise |
| 5 | Enquiry progress receipt | A buyer is unsure whether a form was submitted | Contact forms often give little follow-up clarity | High | Medium | Consent + success state; duplicate-safe; no extra PII |
| 6 | Case-study outcome ledger | Metrics can look unsupported or stale | Profile claims lack evidence links | High | Medium | Metric shown only with source, time range, and owner approval |
| 7 | Project comparison view | Buyers struggle to see relevant stacks and constraints | Marketplaces mix unrelated skills | Medium | Low | Compare only verified public project facts |
| 8 | Service fit and exclusions page | Buyers may assume services outside capacity | Broad provider directories invite mismatch | High | Low | Scope boundaries and handoff/refusal path are clear |
| 9 | Downloadable proposal outline | First call repeats basic discovery questions | Marketplace quoting is tied to platform workflow | Medium | Medium | Template includes assumptions and blank owner-approved terms |
| 10 | Accessible low-bandwidth portfolio mode | Heavy media slows mobile review | Portfolios often rely on large visuals | Medium | Medium | Core proof works without motion and with reduced data transfer |
| 11 | Client readiness checklist | Buyers are unsure what access/assets to prepare | Generic listings omit implementation prerequisites | Medium | Low | Checklist never asks for secrets or sensitive data |
| 12 | Project update digest (opt-in) | Interested buyers lose track of relevant releases | Marketplace feed noise obscures a specific provider | Low | Medium | Double opt-in, unsubscribe, global suppression, no scraped contacts |
| 13 | Review request workflow after verified delivery | Buyers lack a consent-based way to share feedback | Marketplace reviews are platform-bound | Medium | Medium | Request only after real delivery; no incentive/fake review; owner approval |
| 14 | Service ROI worksheet | Buyers need to compare a project cost with current manual work | Marketplace quotes rarely model customer's own baseline | Medium | Medium | User-provided inputs only; assumptions visible; no guaranteed savings |
| 15 | Client handoff and maintenance guide | Projects can be hard to operate after delivery | Platform focuses on hire/payment rather than ownership handoff | High | Medium | Includes ownership, runbook, support boundary and client acceptance |

No item in this proposed roadmap is implemented by being listed here. Do not add online checkout until service, price, legal terms, delivery capacity, tax handling, and refund/support rules are owner-approved.
