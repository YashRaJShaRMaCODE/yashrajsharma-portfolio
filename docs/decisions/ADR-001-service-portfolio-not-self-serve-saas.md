# ADR-001: Keep the portfolio a service lead channel

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-17

## Context

The current repository presents project-based product engineering services and captures optional mailing-list signups. It has no self-serve product, account provisioning, subscription entitlement, or validated recurring use case.

## Decision

Treat the site as a lead-generation and project-proof portfolio. Revenue comes from owner-approved paid discovery, fixed-scope delivery, and optional maintenance only after scope, price, contract, capacity, tax, refund, and support terms are agreed. Do not add subscription billing or imply automatic client acquisition.

## Consequences

- Keep customer enquiry and mailing-list consent distinct.
- Preserve owner review for public résumé/project claims and all proposals.
- Revisit only after buyer interviews show a repeatable software problem that needs self-serve billing.
