# Portfolio market research

Checked 2026-11-17. This repository is a personal project-engineering services portfolio, not a self-serve SaaS. Marketplace fees and reviews vary by role, country, and time. Public reviews are self-selected and are not a representative survey.

## Target buyer and problem hypothesis

Likely buyer: an early-stage founder or small product team that needs a bounded web product, workflow automation, or data/reporting engagement and wants one accountable engineer. Pain hypothesis: comparing candidates and defining a safe, deliverable scope takes time. No interviews, qualified leads, signed pilot, or revenue evidence are recorded in this repository.

**Positioning hypothesis:** a direct portfolio that helps a buyer inspect shipped demos, understand the work scope, and request a human-reviewed discovery call without joining a freelancer marketplace.

**Offer hypothesis:** paid discovery followed by fixed-scope implementation, with optional maintenance. Pricing, availability, contract, tax, refund, and support terms need owner approval before any quote.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Public price checked | Review-reported friction relevant to this portfolio |

This is a broad marketplace sample, not evidence of how every freelancer is treated. [Trustpilot reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/upwork.com) |

## What this means for the portfolio

- A direct page can make project proof and scope easier to inspect, but it cannot promise more leads or better outcomes than marketplaces.
- The differentiator must be concrete proof, clear scope, an honest availability/response expectation, and a low-friction enquiry—not invented testimonials or urgency.
- Keep the LeadHub form opt-in and limited to contact email/consent/source; never ask people to submit credentials, client data, or confidential project material.
- Keep unverified résumé, employment, project-outcome, and service claims behind owner review.
